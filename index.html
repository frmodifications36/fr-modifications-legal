Last updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FR Modifications, operated by Ragingtuktuk, handles information when you use or interact with the FR Modifications Discord bot.

1. About FR Modifications

FR Modifications is an all-in-one Discord bot providing a variety of features, including logging, moderation, server management, utility features, automation, and other Discord server functionality.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features used, FR Modifications may process information made available through Discord, including:

  • Discord user IDs

FR Modifications aims to process only information reasonably necessary to provide its features, maintain the Bot, prevent abuse, and operate the service.

3. Logging and Moderation

Because FR Modifications provides logging and moderation features, a server may configure the Bot to record certain Discord events. Depending on configuration, this may include moderation actions, deleted or edited messages, member events, role changes, channel changes, or other server events.

Server administrators are responsible for configuring logging features appropriately and informing their members where required.

Information processed by FR Modifications may be used to:

  • Provide the Bot's features.

  • Maintain and improve the Bot's reliability and functionality.

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant Bot features or maintain the service. The amount of information retained and the length of time it is retained may depend on the feature being used and the configuration of the Discord server.

6. Data Sharing
